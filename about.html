About PIVOT

Learn. Create. Build. Improve.

PIVOT is a simple and accessible platform designed to help students learn the basics of web development. Whether you're just starting out or looking to improve your skills, PIVOT gives you the tools, lessons, and practice you need to grow at your own pace.

What is PIVOT?

PIVOT is a programming learning environment focused on web development. It provides easy-to-follow lessons, hands-on practice, and simple tools to help you build real projects. Whether you're learning HTML, CSS, or JavaScript, PIVOT is here to guide you every step of the way.

Why PIVOT Was Created

Many students want to learn coding but don't know where to start, or they feel overwhelmed by complex resources. PIVOT was created to provide a beginner- friendly, organized, and easy-to-use platform that makes web development more approachable, especially for students.
